# The First 90 Days — Gemini graphics prompt sheet

## What to upload to Gemini

Upload a screenshot of the existing calendar desk and the relevant reference sheet from `Visuals.zip`. Use the character sheet with Aanya (yellow top, blue trousers, yellow bag) when a human is needed. Use one consistent character across these assets. If the project team intends another named lead, choose that reference first and keep the same appearance throughout.

These are illustrations for fictional situations drawn from the exhibit's themes. They must not look like participant photographs, research records or measured outcomes.

## Paste this art direction before each numbered prompt

Create one production asset for Studio Bridge's interactive exhibit THE FIRST 90 DAYS. Match the uploaded Studio Bridge illustrations: bold dark contours, confident flat shapes, lightly imperfect drawn lines, warm cream paper, bright blue, pink, yellow and mint accents. The setting is a junior designer's working desk. Make the objects feel handled and specific, not like generic office stock icons. Use the same visual style and proportions as the uploaded character and object references.

Deliver one isolated asset on a genuinely transparent RGBA PNG background. No white rectangle, checkerboard pattern, coloured backdrop, new desk surface, scene border, page frame, watermark or mockup. Keep every part inside the image with approximately 8% clear padding. Render at least 1600 pixels on the longest edge. Use a near-overhead view with only a slight shallow angle, consistent across the set. Use small contact shadows within the object; do not paint a large floor shadow. Keep warm light coming from the upper left.

Do not generate readable text, letters, numbers, fake logos, labels, interface screens or dialogue. Leave appropriate blank areas for live text that will be added in the website. Do not add Bridge and Arch, confetti, ornamental stars or unrelated people. The asset must help show a change in the work. Supply a single asset, never a contact sheet. Where a numbered brief asks for multiple layers, generate EACH listed layer separately in a separate response, with the same view and scale. Do not bake the complete interaction into one flattened illustration.
